5. The current pick: Sealed Backtest

An AI research agent iterates SOL/USDC trading strategies on real Kraken data. The eval and the out-of-sample holdout are committed on Solana before iteration 1, and a strategy only counts if it survives the sealed holdout. Built: all six race handlers, Sealed loop, prereg, devnet memo anchor, /verify, pay_usdc 400-point path. New work: presentation plus one guardrail beat.

Demo numbers (visible score → sealed holdout score)

The red-then-green moment: the champion on the backtest it was tuned on falls below even the naive baseline on the sealed holdout; the modest trend filter holds and improves.

"Sealed also…" (one spoken line each)

  • Commits every attempt, not just the winner (#199, #271): each round's candidates hashed into one Merkle root on-chain, so p-hacking is visible.
  • Pays only for results that generalise (#35): escrowed USDC releases on a GENERALISES verdict.
  • Refunds buyers of overfit strategies (#89): refund rate becomes a public trust score.
  • Is the check that releases agent-to-agent escrow (#160, #165): when both sides are machines, the pre-committed holdout decides.
  • Seals the agent's spend policy and tool allow-list before it runs (#147, #194, #259): verifiable guardrails, not promises. Over-cap action blocked pending human co-sign.
  • Can run a sealed API league table (#189): vendors cannot tune to a probe set they cannot see.
  • Works for any scored model (#19, tokenomics, epidemic): domain = three plug-in files.

Pitch line, in the organisers' own words

"Every AI-found trading edge looks great on the backtest it was tuned on. Ours locks the test on Solana before the agent starts, and anything that only fits the past is rejected in public. An AI x Solana application for provenance and verification that improves research workflows: funds and allocators pay per attested backtest."

Track text: "accelerate scientific discovery, improve research workflows, address institutional challenges, or explore AI x Solana applications such as agent payments, identity, provenance and verification." Organiser ideas echoed: #259 "how do you stop it chasing a bug that looks like a discovery?", #35 "agreeing in advance what counts as a successful replication", #160 "who decides a result failed when both sides are machines?"

Likely judge questions

Question (who)Answer
Why a chain? A database could store the hash. (Gvantsa, Cap, Ali)A database owner can backdate. Commit-reveal needs a public timestamp that nobody, including us, can rewrite. The same memo transfer also settles USDC, so the attestation and the payment are one transaction.
Who is liable when the agent loses money? (Gvantsa)Nobody trades: it is research evidence, not trading advice, devnet only. The product is the attestation; capital decisions stay with the human, and any agent action above the cap needs a human co-sign.
What can the agent sign? (Ali)Only memo transfers under the Spending Controls cap (per-call and total budget). Anything above it is blocked pending human approval, shown live. Keys are throwaway devnet keypairs.
Isn't the overfit moment staged? (Dean, Ali)We seeded a naive grid search on purpose; that is what most backtests are. The point is that the oracle catches it, and the holdout was committed before the search ran: click the explorer link and compare the timestamps.
Who pays, and who are the first 100 users? (Udai, Cap)Funds and allocators doing diligence on agent strategies, plus signal sellers and copy-trading platforms who want a trust badge. Pay per attested backtest through x402.
Can't the agent just peek at the holdout? (Ali, Udai)It never has it: the holdout is salted and only its hash is on-chain until reveal. The verify page re-hashes the bundle; edit one line and it turns red.
Is this investment advice / regulated? (Gvantsa)No. It certifies how a result was tested, not what to buy; devnet only, labelled "research evidence".
Is it just a wrapper around an LLM? (Udai, Gvantsa)The LLM proposes; the locked eval and the sealed oracle decide, with numbers, not LLM opinion. The harness is infrastructure: any scored environment plugs in with three files.
Who is the user, and is the UX usable? (Delz)A strategy author gets a shareable verify link; a buyer clicks it and sees green or red. One page, no wallet needed to read it.
Will you keep building? (Cap)Yes: next plug-ins are token-design stress tests for launchpads and the bounty vault; the loop comes from Adrian's AI Inventor research pipeline.
